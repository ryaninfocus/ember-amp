EMBER AMP - Windows

INSTALL
1. Run "Ember-Amp-...-Windows-Setup.exe".
2. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC": click "More info", then "Run anyway".
   (This happens because Ember Amp isn't signed with a paid certificate. It's a one-time step.)
3. Follow the installer. It puts:
     - Ember Amp (the app) in Program Files, with a Start menu shortcut
     - the VST3 plugin in C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3 (Ableton, Reaper, Cubase, FL Studio...)

FIRST LAUNCH
- Open Ember Amp from the Start menu.
- Click SETTINGS (bottom right) > Audio Settings and pick your audio interface.
  For the lowest delay, choose "Windows Audio (Exclusive Mode)" as the device type.
- Pick which input your guitar is plugged into at the bottom of the window.

Needs 64-bit Windows 10 or 11.
