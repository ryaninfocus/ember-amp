## Download

| Your computer | Download |
|---|---|
| **Mac** (Apple Silicon or Intel, macOS 11+) | `Ember-Amp-…-Mac.dmg` |
| **Windows** (64-bit Windows 10/11) | `Ember-Amp-…-Windows-Setup.exe` |
| Windows, no installer | `Ember-Amp-…-Windows-Portable.zip` |

No account or sign-in needed. Just download and install.

## What you get
- **Ember Amp app**: plug your guitar into your audio interface and play
- **VST3 plugin**: Ableton, Reaper, Cubase, FL Studio, Studio One…
- **AU plugin** (Mac): Logic, GarageBand

4 amps (Clean, Crunch, Distortion, Solo) with 5 presets each, 8 pedals (gate, compressor, overdrive, distortion, pitch, chorus, delay, reverb), a tuner, IR loading and your own saved presets.

## First time opening it
Ember Amp isn't registered with Apple or Microsoft, so your computer asks once:
- **Mac:** if the installer won't open, go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**.
- **Windows:** if you see "Windows protected your PC", click **More info → Run anyway**.

Then open **Options → Audio Settings** in Ember Amp and pick your audio interface.
