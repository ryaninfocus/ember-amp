EMBER AMP - Mac

INSTALL
1. Double-click "Install Ember Amp.pkg".
2. If your Mac says it can't be opened because the developer can't be verified:
   open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click "Open Anyway"
   next to the Ember Amp message. Then open the installer again.
   (This happens because Ember Amp isn't registered with Apple. It's a one-time step.)
3. Follow the installer. It puts:
     - Ember Amp (the app) in your Applications folder
     - the AU plugin (Logic, GarageBand) and VST3 plugin (Ableton, Reaper, Cubase...)

FIRST LAUNCH
- Open Ember Amp from Applications (or search "Ember Amp" in Spotlight / Raycast).
- Allow microphone access when asked - that's how the app hears your guitar.
- Click SETTINGS (bottom right) > Audio Settings and pick your audio interface.
- Pick which input your guitar is plugged into at the bottom of the window.

Works on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs, macOS 11 or newer.
